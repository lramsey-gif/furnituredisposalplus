Donation pickup

Furniture donation pickup

Donation pickup works like standard removal, with usable furniture taken to a local donation center first. You get the piece out of the house without renting a truck, and the item gets a second life instead of a landfill. Pricing starts at $90.

How it works

Donation-first, disposal last

Every pickup follows the same order: donate what is usable, recycle what is recyclable, dispose of only what remains. Your Loaders assess the piece on site and route it accordingly.

What donates well

  • Couches and chairs with no major tears, stains, or broken frames
  • Solid wood dressers, tables, desks, and bookshelves
  • Complete dining sets and bed frames with hardware

What usually cannot be donated

  • Mattresses in most regions, which go to recycling instead where programs exist
  • Damaged particleboard furniture
  • Heavily soiled or pet-damaged upholstery

Donation acceptance is decided by the receiving center, not by us, so it cannot be guaranteed in advance. Either way, the item leaves your home on the day you book.
