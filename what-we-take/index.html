Not accepted

What we cannot take

A short list, mostly for safety and transport rules:

  • Full propane tanks, fuel, paint, and other hazardous liquids
  • Wet or actively leaking items
  • Items requiring plumbing, gas, or electrical disconnection that has not been done

Empty propane tanks, electronics, and appliances are accepted as additional items. When in doubt, note it in the booking and the team confirms before service day.
