Questions

Common questions

How much does furniture removal cost?

Pricing starts at $90 for the first item and additional items start at $20. You see the exact price for your specific item and address before you book, and the price shown is the price you pay.

Do I need to be home for the pickup?

No. Leave the item in an accessible spot such as a driveway, porch, garage, or curb, note the location when booking, and your Loaders pick it up without you present.

How fast can you pick up my furniture?

Same-day and next-day pickup is available in most areas across all 50 states. Available windows show when you enter your item and location.

What happens to my old furniture?

Usable furniture is taken to local donation centers where possible. The rest is disposed of responsibly, with recycling prioritized over landfill.

Do you take more than one piece?

Yes. Add multiple items to one booking. The first item starts at $90 and each additional item starts at $20.

Do your Loaders do the lifting?

Yes. Your Loaders handle all lifting and loading, including from inside the home, upstairs, or down a staircase. You do not need to move anything.

Are there stair, fuel, or hidden fees?

No. The upfront price includes labor, loading, hauling, and disposal or donation drop-off. There are no fuel surcharges and no stair fees.

Is Furniture Disposal Plus part of LoadUp?

Yes. Furniture Disposal Plus is a brand of LoadUp Technologies, LLC, founded in 2014 and headquartered in Alpharetta, Georgia.

How do I pay?

You pay online when you book. Nothing is owed in person, and there is no cash handling on service day.

What areas do you serve?

All 50 states through a nationwide network of vetted independent Loaders. Enter your address at booking to confirm local availability.
