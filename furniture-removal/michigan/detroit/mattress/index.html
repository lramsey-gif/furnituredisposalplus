Local know-how

How mattress disposal works in Detroit

Detroit collects bulk on your weekly trash day, funded by the annual solid waste fee, and mattresses are accepted, though they must be set out at the curb on your collection day. Michigan has no statewide mattress recycling law, so there is no producer-funded program, and the weekly bulk route or a hauler are the main paths.

A mattress is roughly eighty percent recyclable once broken down, springs to scrap steel and foam to carpet padding, though much depends on the disposal route. A queen weighs sixty to a hundred pounds and does not fit in most cars, and the winter haul out to the curb is harder with snow and ice on the ground.

A booked Furniture Disposal Plus pickup skips the curb timing and the winter haul. Your Loaders come to the mattress wherever it sits, up from a basement or out of an upstairs bedroom, on a window you choose, often same-day or next-day across Detroit ZIP codes, and it routes to recycling where facilities exist.
