Local know-how

How bulky disposal works in California

California runs the Bye Bye Mattress program, a statewide mattress recycling system funded at point of sale, so mattresses are routed to recyclers rather than landfill. A booked pickup replaces all of that: your Loaders arrive in the window you choose, carry the piece out from anywhere in the home, and you never touch a schedule, sticker, or transfer station.

Many California cities also enforce strict illegal dumping fines, which makes a documented pickup the safer path for renters and landlords. What cannot be donated is recycled where facilities exist, and only the remainder is disposed of responsibly.

Pricing in California starts at $90 for the first item and $20 for each additional piece, with all labor, stairs, loading, and disposal included. Same-day and next-day windows are available in most California ZIP codes.
