Local know-how

How furniture disposal works in Frederick

The City of Frederick gives each household one free bulk item pickup per year, and it is generous within its limits: you may set out roughly ten items on that annual free appointment. You submit a request through the city's online portal, listing each item in advance, and place everything where your household trash normally goes. Crews will not enter houses, fences, or private property.

Appliances are the catch. Because the city dropped its citywide bulk program for white goods, refrigerators, washers, dryers, and similar appliances are not collected on the regular bulk appointment; they require a separate fee-based arrangement, with refrigerant removal the owner's responsibility. Tires are not collected at all and go to the county landfill, and additional pickups beyond the one free appointment are fee-based.

A booked Furniture Disposal Plus pickup is not limited to one free trip a year. Your Loaders come to the piece wherever it sits, out of the basement or down from a second floor, on a window you choose, often same-day or next-day across Frederick ZIP codes. There is no annual cap, no separate appliance arrangement, and no item list to file, and Furniture Disposal Plus serves Frederick and the wider county from Urbana to Middletown and Walkersville.

Frederick questions

Furniture removal in Frederick, common questions

How fast can you pick up furniture in Frederick?

Same-day and next-day windows are available across most Frederick ZIP codes, from Baker Park and Downtown Frederick to the Golden Mile and Ballenger Creek. Availability shows when you enter your item and address.

Does the City of Frederick pick up furniture at the curb?

Yes, one free bulk pickup a year, up to about ten items, requested through the online portal, but appliances are excluded and need a separate paid arrangement. Furniture Disposal Plus carries the piece from inside on a window you choose, with no annual cap and no item list to file.

What happens to my furniture after pickup in Frederick?

Usable pieces go to local donation partners first, including Goodwill, the Salvation Army, and Habitat for Humanity ReStore. What cannot be donated is recycled where facilities exist, and only the remainder is disposed of responsibly. Donation acceptance is decided by the receiving center.

How do I get rid of a mattress in Frederick?

A mattress can go out on your one free annual bulk pickup, listed in advance through the online portal. Furniture Disposal Plus takes the mattress from inside your home on a window you choose, so you never use up your free trip or file a request.
