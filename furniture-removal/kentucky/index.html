How bulky disposal works in Kentucky

Louisville runs scheduled junk set-out periods by route, and setting furniture out early is a citable offense. A booked pickup replaces all of that: your Loaders arrive in the window you choose, carry the piece out from anywhere in the home, and you never touch a schedule, sticker, or transfer station.

Kentucky donation centers welcome solid wood pieces, and Loaders route usable furniture to them first. What cannot be donated is recycled where facilities exist, and only the remainder is disposed of responsibly.

Pricing in Kentucky starts at $90 for the first item and $20 for each additional piece, with all labor, stairs, loading, and disposal included. Same-day and next-day windows are available in most Kentucky ZIP codes.
