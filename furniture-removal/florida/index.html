Local know-how

How bulky disposal works in Florida

Many Florida cities run weekly or biweekly bulk pickup, but rules on what counts as bulk versus construction debris trip people up, and mixed piles get skipped. A booked pickup replaces all of that: your Loaders arrive in the window you choose, carry the piece out from anywhere in the home, and you never touch a schedule, sticker, or transfer station.

Humidity and afternoon rain ruin curb-left upholstery fast, so scheduled indoor pickup beats the curb gamble. What cannot be donated is recycled where facilities exist, and only the remainder is disposed of responsibly.

Pricing in Florida starts at $90 for the first item and $20 for each additional piece, with all labor, stairs, loading, and disposal included. Same-day and next-day windows are available in most Florida ZIP codes.
