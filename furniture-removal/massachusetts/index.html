How bulky disposal works in Massachusetts

Massachusetts banned mattresses and textiles from disposal statewide, so mattresses picked up here are routed to recyclers, not the landfill. A booked pickup replaces all of that: your Loaders arrive in the window you choose, carry the piece out from anywhere in the home, and you never touch a schedule, sticker, or transfer station.
