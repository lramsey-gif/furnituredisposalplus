Local know-how

How washer and dryer disposal works in Austin

Austin gives residents a free way to get rid of a washer. Austin Resource Recovery offers two free bulk pickups a year that accept appliances, and unlike a refrigerator, a washer has no refrigerant to remove first, so there is no certified-recovery step. In 2025 the city moved from a fixed bulk schedule to an on-demand system, so a pickup has to be requested and scheduled ahead.

The catch is timing and the lift. The bulk pickup runs on the city's schedule, the machine has to be drained and set at the curb by the pickup date, and it cannot go out more than a day early without a fine. A washer is roughly three-quarters steel, so once collected it routes to scrap-metal recycling. There is no utility rebate for a washer, those programs cover working refrigerators and freezers only.

A booked Furniture Disposal Plus pickup skips the bulk appointment and the curb set-out. Your Loaders come to the washer wherever it sits, on a window you choose, often same-day or next-day across Austin ZIP codes, and the steel is recycled. Furniture Disposal Plus serves Austin and the metro from Round Rock to Cedar Park and Pflugerville.
