How bulky disposal works in Delaware

Delaware's small footprint means most pickups route through a handful of transfer stations, keeping windows tight and same-day service common. A booked pickup replaces all of that: your Loaders arrive in the window you choose, carry the piece out from anywhere in the home, and you never touch a schedule, sticker, or transfer station.

Usable furniture is offered to donation centers in the Wilmington area before anything heads to disposal. What cannot be donated is recycled where facilities exist, and only the remainder is disposed of responsibly.

Pricing in Delaware starts at $90 for the first item and $20 for each additional piece, with all labor, stairs, loading, and disposal included. Same-day and next-day windows are available in most Delaware ZIP codes.
