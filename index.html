Questions

Common questions

How much does furniture removal cost?

Pricing starts at $90 for the first item and additional items start at $20. You see the exact price for your specific item before you book, and the price shown is the price you pay.

Do I need to be home for the pickup?

No. You can leave the item in an accessible spot such as a driveway, porch, or garage, and your Loaders will pick it up without you present.

How fast can you pick up my furniture?

Same-day and next-day pickup is available in most areas across all 50 states. Availability shows when you enter your item and location.

What happens to my old furniture?

Usable furniture is taken to local donation centers where possible. The rest is disposed of responsibly, with recycling prioritized over landfill.

Do you take more than one piece?

Yes. You can add multiple items to one booking. The first item starts at $90 and each additional item starts at $20.

Do your Loaders do the lifting?

Yes. Your Loaders handle all the lifting and loading, including from inside the home, upstairs, or down a staircase. You do not need to move anything yourself.
