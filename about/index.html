The company

A LoadUp Technologies brand

Furniture Disposal Plus focuses on one job: getting furniture out of homes and offices with an exact price shown before booking. It is built and operated by LoadUp Technologies, LLC, the Alpharetta, Georgia company behind goloadup.com, founded in 2014.

Pickups are performed by a nationwide network of vetted independent Loaders. Loaders are background-checked, reviewed after every job, and equipped to handle stairs, disassembly, and tight clearances.

How the technology works

Booking and dispatch run on the Workmon.ai platform. Item-level pricing means the quote is generated from the piece of furniture itself, not from a truck volume estimate, so the price you see holds on service day.

Where furniture goes

Donation first, recycling second, disposal last. Usable pieces go to local donation centers, metal and mattresses are recycled where programs exist, and only the remainder is disposed of responsibly.
