Privacy policy

This policy describes what information Furniture Disposal Plus collects when you visit the site or book a pickup, how it is used to deliver the service, and the choices you have. Furniture Disposal Plus is a brand of LoadUp Technologies, LLC.

Information we collect

When you book a pickup we collect the information needed to perform it: your name, contact details, service address, the items booked, and payment information processed by our payment provider. When you browse the site we collect standard analytics data such as pages viewed and general location.

How we use it

  • To schedule, price, and complete your pickup
  • To send booking confirmations and service-day updates
  • To improve the site and our service coverage

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. Service details are shared with the Loaders performing your pickup and with the platforms that operate booking and payment, and only to the extent needed to complete the job.

Data retention and choices

Booking records are kept as required for accounting and support. You may request access to or deletion of your personal information by contacting us through the contact page.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the contact page. Furniture Disposal Plus is a brand of LoadUp Technologies, LLC, Alpharetta, Georgia.
