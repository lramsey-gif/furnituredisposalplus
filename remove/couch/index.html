Questions

Couch removal questions

Do you remove sleeper sofas?

Yes. Sleeper sofas are heavier than standard couches because of the fold-out frame, so mention it when booking and your Loaders come prepared. The starting price may reflect the extra weight.

Can you get a couch out of a basement or upstairs apartment?

Yes. Your Loaders handle stairs, elevators, and tight turns at no extra charge. Note anything unusual in the booking so the team brings the right equipment.

Will my couch be donated?

If it is in usable condition with no major tears, stains, or broken frames, your Loaders take it to a local donation center first. Otherwise it is recycled or disposed of responsibly.

Do you take couches with pet damage or stains?

Yes. Condition does not affect whether we take it, only where it ends up. Damaged couches are recycled or disposed of instead of donated.

Can the couch be left outside or in the garage?

Yes. If the couch is in an accessible spot like a garage, porch, or driveway, you do not need to be home for the pickup.

Do you disassemble couches that will not fit through the door?

Loaders remove legs and tilt the couch on end, which handles most doorways. Full frame disassembly is not typically needed for a standard couch.
