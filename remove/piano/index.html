Questions

Piano removal questions

Can you move a piano down stairs?

Yes. Your Loaders handle stairs, tight turns, and doorways at no extra charge. Note the number of flights when you book.

Do you take both upright and grand pianos?

Yes. Uprights, spinets, consoles, baby grands, and grands are all handled. Note the type and size when booking.

Can a playable piano be donated instead of dumped?

Where a piano is in playable condition, we route it to donation where a receiving partner exists rather than disposal.

How much does piano removal cost?

Pricing varies by size, weight, and access. Your exact price shows before you book, with no on-site estimate.

Do I need to be home for the pickup?

If the piano is in an accessible spot and access is arranged, you do not need to be home for the pickup.

Can you remove an organ or digital piano too?

Yes. Console organs and digital pianos are taken along with acoustic pianos. Note the type when booking.
