Questions

Dresser removal questions

Do I need to empty the dresser?

Yes, please remove clothing and personal items before pickup. Drawers can stay in the dresser, and your Loaders secure them for the carry.

Do you take dressers with mirrors attached?

Yes. Mention the mirror when booking. Your Loaders detach it if needed and haul both pieces, with the mirror counted as part of the same item where practical.

What if my dresser is particleboard and falling apart?

It is still accepted. Damaged particleboard furniture is disposed of responsibly since donation centers rarely take it.

Does the mirror count as a separate item?

No. An attached or matching dresser mirror is picked up as part of the dresser at no additional charge.

Do drawers need to be removed or taped?

No. Just empty them. Loaders secure the drawers for the carry and the drive.

Do you take particle board or IKEA-style dressers?

Yes. Flat-pack furniture is fine in any condition, including pieces that are already partially disassembled.
