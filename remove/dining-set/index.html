Questions

Dining Set removal questions

Is the price for the whole set?

The starting price covers the table, and chairs are added as additional items from $20 each or bundled, with the exact total shown before you confirm.

Do you take china cabinets and buffets with the set?

Yes. Add them to the same booking as additional items and everything goes in one pickup.

Can you take just the chairs?

Yes. Book the exact pieces you want removed. There is no requirement to take the full set.

Can you take just the table or just the chairs?

Yes. Partial sets are fine. A table alone or chairs alone are priced as their own items at booking.

Do you take glass-top tables?

Yes. Loaders carry the glass separately from the base and pad it for transport.

What about a china cabinet or hutch with the set?

Add it at booking and it is picked up in the same trip. Hutches are carried in two pieces when the top separates.
