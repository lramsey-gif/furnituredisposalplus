Questions

Patio Furniture removal questions

Do you take rusted or weathered patio furniture?

Yes. Condition does not matter. Metal pieces are recycled, and usable sets are donated.

Can you pick up from the backyard if I am not home?

Yes. Leave the items accessible, note the location when booking, and your Loaders handle it without you present.

Do you take grills and fire pits with the furniture?

Yes. Add them as additional items. Empty propane tanks are accepted, but full tanks cannot be transported.

Do you take glass-top patio tables?

Yes. Loaders carry the glass separately and pad it for transport, even if the glass is cracked.

Can you take a concrete umbrella base?

Yes. Umbrella bases and other heavy patio accessories are picked up with the set. Mention them at booking.

Do you take rusted or weather-damaged furniture?

Yes. Condition does not matter. Rusted metal frames are recycled as scrap rather than landfilled.
