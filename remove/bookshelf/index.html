Questions

Bookshelf removal questions

Does the bookshelf need to be empty?

Yes. Please remove books and decor first. Fixed shelves can stay in place, and loose shelves can be stacked inside or beside the unit.

Do you take wall-mounted shelving?

Yes, once it is off the wall. Your Loaders haul detached shelving, but unmounting from the wall is not part of the standard pickup.

Is particleboard shelving accepted?

Yes. Particleboard units in any condition are picked up, though they typically go to disposal rather than donation.

Do you take bookshelves anchored to the wall?

Yes. Loaders remove anti-tip straps and wall anchors during pickup. Wall patching is not included.

Can you take an entertainment center or wall unit?

Yes. Large wall units are priced upfront at booking and disassembled on site when needed.

Do the shelves need to come out first?

No. Fixed shelves stay in, and Loaders remove and bag adjustable shelves themselves.
